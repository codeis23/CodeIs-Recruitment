Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you wanted to know about how Codeis Club works.

Do I need prior coding or design experience to apply? +
No! We care far more about your curiosity, consistency, and excitement to get your hands dirty than a fancy resume. Many of our strongest developers and designers started with zero prior knowledge.
Can 1st year (freshmen) students join? +
Yes, absolutely. In fact, over 60% of our recruitment intake is dedicated to 1st and 2nd years so that you get full peer mentorship throughout your campus journey.
What is the expected weekly time commitment? +
You can expect to spend a few hours a week collaborating with your squad on projects and hackathons. We value quality over endless meetings.
Can I apply for multiple tracks if I have multidisciplinary interests? +
Yes! You can choose your primary track in the application form and mention any secondary interests (e.g. UI Design + Frontend, or IoT + AI) in your vibe check responses.
Are there membership fees or club dues? +
A Small Nominal Fee Will Be Taken Only For Selected Candidates
